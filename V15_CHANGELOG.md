# Version 15: The War Desk

## Designed for students aged 16 to 17

**Welcome screen:** One folder, three genuine historical photographs, a clear title and a prominent Start button. Secondary Home and Restart controls are hidden on the welcome screen to reduce clutter.

**Every decision:** A brief scenario, one direct question and two large answers. A short historical note is available on request. The accompanying archive photo retains its date, location and credit; longer image descriptions are available in the GPO photo viewer.

**Every outcome:** Short explanation, historic film or tactical animation and a single clear way forward. Redundant media type labels have been removed.

**Specific imagery:** President Nasser is pictured on the Straits of Tiran and Jerusalem Under Fire decision cards, with accurate labels indicating that his photograph is illustrative historical context and not imagery of the fighting in Jerusalem.

**Preserved systems:** 14 decision stages, correct historical routes, 2 spare lives and Game Over on the third mistake, restart, progress saving, 1967 map units, 8 video sequences and the original Hebrew audio only in the Old City clip.

**Sources:** Photographic attributions, archive viewer and educator notes remain in the package.
