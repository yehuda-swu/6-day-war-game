# Version 20: Home screen soundtrack

The game tries to play the supplied military march as soon as the home screen loads. Audible autoplay is blocked by some browsers until a visitor interacts with the page. Where autoplay is blocked, the game now shows a short notice and begins the soundtrack on the first tap, click, or activation key on the home screen, without requiring Start Mission. The existing Play soundtrack button remains available.

The audio continues without restarting across decisions and maps, pauses only for the Old City Hebrew film, and resumes afterwards. Disabling music remains respected. All Version 19 game content and clear outcome screens are retained.
