# Animated map documentation

Six original SVG animations are included in `assets/maps/`. They use Natural Earth low resolution country boundaries as a modern geographical base. Natural Earth is public domain.

All line markings, arrows, city plans, highlights and annotations are schematic educational reconstructions, **not surveyed military movements, precise 1967 borders or legal boundary determinations**. The schematic Jerusalem plan is expressly not a street map.

Stages 1 and 3 illustrate military preparedness and perceived threats, not a claim that coordinated invasions had already begun. Stage 12 represents areas under Israeli control after the ceasefire, including Sinai, Gaza, the West Bank and Golan. Stage 13 distinguishes the 19 June cabinet deliberations concerning Sinai and Golan from wider Israeli debates about the West Bank and Jerusalem.

All animations are silent and designed to run in a browser without external JavaScript libraries.
