# Version 12 map and flag sources

Six original silent SVG maps live under `assets/maps/`. Unit silhouettes, arrows and flags were drawn specifically for this educational game and do not represent precise historical formations or routes.

## Geographical base

The modern Natural Earth low resolution geography is used as the public domain base map. All war fronts and territorial colours are schematic. Boundaries do not establish 1967 legal status.

## 1967 national flags

* **Egypt** retained the United Arab Republic flag in 1967: red, white and black horizontal stripes with **two green stars**. Confirmation from Egypt's presidency: https://www.presidency.eg/en/%D9%85%D8%B5%D8%B1/%D9%85%D8%B9%D9%84%D9%88%D9%85%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D8%A3%D8%B3%D8%A7%D8%B3%D9%8A%D8%A9/%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B9%D9%84%D9%85-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%88%D8%B7%D9%86%D9%89/%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B9%D9%84%D9%85-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D8%B5%D8%B1%D9%8A-%D9%81%D9%8A-%D8%B9%D9%87%D8%AF-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AB%D9%88%D8%B1%D8%A9/
* **Syria**, from 1963 to 1972, used red, white and black stripes with **three green stars**, not the flag used in 2026: https://crwflags.com/FotW/FLAGS/sy-his2.html
* **Israel** uses the blue Star of David between blue stripes on white.
* **Jordan** uses the black, white and green stripes, red triangle and a white star.
* **UN** uses a pale blue field and is labelled UN for easy recognition.

The flag icons are small graphic simplifications rather than official reproduced digital flag artwork. They are attached to infantry, tanks, artillery, aircraft and ship icons wherever relevant.

## Presentation safeguards

* 1967 country flags, not contemporary variants
* Military unit movements are **illustrative reconstructions**, not literal film records
* No audio or invented battle casualties
* The land and peace stage displays positional symbols rather than implying postwar troop advances
* Animated icons must not be treated as a quantitative order of battle
