# Version 18: Music from the home screen

The original Version 17 cinematic score has been replaced with the military march supplied by the user.

* Removed approximately five seconds of silence at the start so the music begins promptly.
* Kept the rest of the supplied recording and enabled looping.
* Attempts playback immediately on the home screen. Browsers that block automatic sound show a Play soundtrack button on the home screen.
* The first user click anywhere on the welcome screen can also enable playback.
* Music continues through decisions, map animations and other archive videos.
* Music pauses for the Old City liberation video, preserving the original Hebrew audio, then resumes.
* Music can be turned off and on without losing playback position.
* All existing decision flow, source credits and final screen controls are retained.

Audio attribution: user supplied recording titled *March of the Israeli Defence Force*. Before publishing the game, confirm the recording and composition may be reused and publicly hosted.
