## Checklist prior to publication

1. Confirm the video rights holder for each item and obtain permission for public distribution, including edited extracts and stills.
2. Confirm requirements for naming, logos, source URL and any restrictions on alterations.
3. Keep the credit statements attached to the clips and in this log.
4. Clearly distinguish archive footage from reconstructions and graphics made for the game.
5. If rights are not available, remove the relevant videos and stills. The game will show its fallback cards.
