# Current media register | Version 15

## Decision screen archive cards

* **01:** UN flag being lowered at King's Gate, near Gaza City, **19 May 1967**. **UN Photo**. File `assets/archive/01-unef-flag-19-may-1967.jpg`. Supplied by the project owner.
* **05:** Wreckage of Egyptian aircraft on the first day of the war, **5 June 1967**. **Dan Hadani Collection, The Pritzker Family National Photography Collection, The National Library of Israel**. File `assets/archive/05-egyptian-aircraft-wreckage.jpg`.
* **09:** Israeli soldiers using a makeshift shower in Sinai, 1967. **Dan Hadani Collection, The Pritzker Family National Photography Collection, The National Library of Israel**. File `assets/archive/09-israeli-soldiers-sinai-shower.jpg`.
* **02 and 06:** Contextual 1967 photograph of President Nasser, **Government Press Office (GPO) collection**. Precise day and location unconfirmed. Stage 06 is specifically labelled as context, not a picture of the fighting in Jerusalem.
* **04, 10 and 11:** Relevant contemporary photographs from the **Government Press Office (GPO)** collection, with their source details in the decision panels and archive gallery.
* **08:** Historical IDF Old City film still, credited to **IDF**.
* **14:** President Nasser arriving at Khartoum on **30 August 1967**; a film still from **AP Archive**. It does not show the adoption of the summit declaration.
* **03, 07, 12 and 13:** Historical photographs from the user supplied **Government Press Office (GPO)** collection, with exact dates identified as unverified when appropriate.

## Outcome footage and audio

* Edited documentary clips: Stages 04, 05, 06, 10 and 11, **Unpacked / OpenDor Media**. Presenter shots removed, audio silent.
* Stage 08, **IDF**, original Hebrew communications preserved; the only video soundtrack in the game.
