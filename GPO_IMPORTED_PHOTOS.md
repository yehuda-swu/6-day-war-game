# Version 14: supplied archive photographs

The user supplied a 34 image GPO archive package. 32 photographs are used in the game. The 1967 newspaper front page and the unrelated Nixon photograph have been excluded as they do not suit the historical decisions or their rights are distinct.

The iconic photograph of the three paratroopers at the Western Wall is credited to David Rubinger; the copy came from the supplied Government Press Office archive. The photographs on the home screen are arranged as a designed collage. The folder and TOP SECRET stamp are theatrical design elements, not genuine archival objects.

Photographs with an unverified exact shooting date are marked accordingly. Other image sources such as UN Photo, AP Archive, and the National Library of Israel retain their distinct original attribution.

Every imported photograph is stored locally under assets/gpo/ and appears in the gallery for the relevant stage. The source manifest is GPO_IMPORTED_PHOTOS.csv.
