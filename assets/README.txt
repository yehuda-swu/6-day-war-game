Six Days of Decisions media folder

Add your SWU UK and Explore logos, then the numbered JPG and MP4 files listed in ../MEDIA_CHECKLIST.md.

The video plays automatically after the historically accurate choice, then takes the student to the next decision when finished.

Missing JPG or MP4 files are supported; the game will use a visual outcome card instead.

Upload only files with permission to publish on your public GitHub Pages website.
