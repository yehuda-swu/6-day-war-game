# GPO photographic archive catalogue

Source gallery: https://www.sixdaywar.org/resources-documents-photos-and-video/photos/

The user confirmed permission to use Government Press Office (GPO) photographs.
All GPO images are credited to the Government Press Office, with known individual photographers named in the game.

The original webpage lists image captions but direct downloads are blocked in this environment.
The game therefore shows independently verified GPO photo images where possible and links remaining photo records back to the original gallery.
Photos dated July or August 1967 are labelled as subsequent historical views and not as live wartime images.
